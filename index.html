Walls
Dimensions & cladding

Wall thickness applies to the three new walls. Cover-board spacing is measured centre to centre and also applies to the existing house façade. The lockpanel drawing shows a drip edge at least 200 mm above the drawn grade; it is not a construction detail.

Stud framing

Stud spacing is centre to centre. Width is along the wall; depth is through the wall. Studs next to corners and openings are positioned to suit the opening.

Roof
Pitch & overhangs

The tiled roof slopes down toward the front window. Overhangs are horizontal; the left and right share one setting. There is no rear overhang at the house. Verify the tile system's minimum pitch and the roof's load capacity before construction.

The white gutter follows the low front eave and drains to the right-hand downspout.

Tile covering

Width is across the roof. Exposed length is measured up the slope between courses, excluding overlap. Profile rise is the height from valley to crown. The Left and Right views show the overlapping course edges.

Rafter framing

Rafter spacing is centre to centre across the roof. Width is across the roof; depth is perpendicular to the slope. The last bay can be narrower.
