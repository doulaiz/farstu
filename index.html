Walls
Dimensions & cladding

Wall thickness applies to the three new walls. Cover-board spacing is measured centre to centre and also applies to the existing house façade. The lockpanel drawing shows a drip edge at least 200 mm above the drawn grade; it is not a construction detail.

New cladding and trim use 22 mm boards in listed retail widths. Set base-board and cover-board widths independently; allow at least 20 mm overlap on each side of the joint. Drawing, lumber takeoff and cladding weight follow these choices.

Stud framing

Stud spacing is centre to centre. Width is along the wall; depth is through the wall. Studs next to corners and openings are positioned to suit the opening.

Studs and rafters use sections from the Derome C24 catalogue: 45 × 70–245 mm in listed depths, or 70 × 70 mm. Changing width filters the available depths. These are stock choices, not load-checked sizes.

Roof
Pitch, width & overhangs

The shed roof slopes down toward the front. Total roof width is independent of slab width. Door-side overhang is the horizontal distance beyond the left slab edge; the opposite overhang equals roof width minus slab width minus door-side overhang. Both sides must stay within 0–1000 mm, so the roof always covers the slab. The opposite overhang is calculated, not necessarily equal.

Tiles below 14° are rejected; tiles from 14° to 22° require a fully sealed high-grade underlayment. Standing-seam metal from 6° to 14° uses double-seamed joints. Manufacturer instructions and project-specific snow, wind, drainage, and building requirements take precedence.

The roof-to-house junction has a standing flashing (ståndskiva); its height is measured above the roof covering. This and the bargeboards and other flashings are schematic; confirm the actual detailing with a roofer. The front eave includes a drip edge, half-round gutter, and right-hand downpipe. Snow retention and roof access equipment are not included; have them specified separately for the site and local requirements.

Tile covering

Used only with the tile covering. Choose 300 mm covering width for Benders Palema (physical size 330 × 420 mm, two waves), or 250 mm for Benders Exklusiv (280 × 420 mm, one wave). Both have 30 mm nominal side overlap. These are product sizes, not a universal standard for concrete or clay tiles.

Exposed length is the batten gauge along the slope: 310–320 mm below 18°, 310–340 mm from 18° to below 22°, and 310–375 mm from 22°. Headlap is 420 mm minus the gauge (45–110 mm). Follow the manufacturer's laying instructions; exposed sites may need tighter spacing. Profile rise is a schematic valley-to-crown height, not the manufacturer's total construction height.

For tiles, total roof width moves in whole covering-width steps (250 or 300 mm); typed widths between steps are rejected. Switching to tiles or changing the tile system aligns roof width to the nearest fitting module, with ties rounded up, while preserving the door-side overhang and slab width. Metal uses 1 mm width steps. The last edge tile adds 30 mm beyond the nominal grid; coordinate that allowance with the verge flashing or special verge tiles before construction. The drawing does not detail this edge allowance.

Rafter framing

Rafter spacing is centre to centre across the roof. Width is across the roof; depth is perpendicular to the slope. The last bay can be narrower.

Method and material assumptions

The slab is treated as supported only by the piers. Every load is shared between the neighbouring pier rows and columns with the lever rule (simple spans in both directions); loads outside the outer piers are cantilevered. Wall weights follow the stud layout, wall height and openings; rafters bear on the front wall and on the selected rear support, so a front overhang adds to the front piers. Drawing layer toggles do not change the estimate; the optional exterior insulation and roof safety/access equipment are excluded.
